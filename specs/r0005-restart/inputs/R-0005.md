## Functional requirements
- Preserve R-0004 source-derived decomposition obligations and dependencies R-0001/R-0003 as unresolved input; verify current touchpoints, bounded graph coverage, subgraph-derived ownership/order, proposed versus realized seams, integrated conformance, runtime seams, and positive/severed production evidence without importing historical approval or completion.
- C first: apply canonical finding normalization, classification and blocking policy after transport/provenance validation; revalidate relied-on historical collector passes append-only from retained evidence.
- E second: preserve typed refusal reason, phase/attempt, committed/observed/pending/uncertain effects, prerequisites and authorized continuation; distinguish wait, artifact correction, setup repair and new authority; stop unchanged failing retries.
- A third: extend canonical graph and wiring closure to all worker/task/phase seams; normal phase completion commits the complete produced and inherited artifact bundle including actual quality strategy bytes, evidence outputs and authority needed by fresh downstream consumers.
- B fourth: share existing lifecycle and persistence facilities for owner/reviewer attempt setup and pickup; reconcile all local records and uncertain host effects before readiness, preserve operation identity and reject changed-input replay.
- D fifth: support many-to-many task-to-criterion contributions, multiple exact proofs per task/criterion and separate joint integration proof; aggregate requirement acceptance independently from task completion and accountable ownership.
- Complete explicit durable continuation from Build through Evaluate, Engineering review, applicable sign-off, Retro and declared Publish/final state under each phase's current authority; one applicable approval policy distinguishes produced work, validated evidence and authorized progression.
- Establish host observation capability before dispatch readiness, preserve exact native contracts and unique run/attempt identity, and converge duplicate events through one observation owner.
- Reuse incumbent graph, evaluation_evidence_edges, wiring_closure, StageLifecycle, RunStore, delivery ports and validators with modest units; retire superseded active coordination per migrated slice and preserve historical compatibility explicitly.
- Report provenance-bound phase/attempt/operation progress, effects, timing, replay/correction counts, missing evidence and provider usage with cache semantics; unavailable usage stays unavailable and replay cannot double-count it.
- Deliver future implementation through bounded PRs with current-source Design, acceptance mapping, six honestly real/simulated-labeled journeys, production-boundary failure evidence and explicit rollback; this stage authors Product only.

## Non-functional requirements (by lens)
- **security**: Treat repository paths, symlinks, selectors, manifests, host events and portable evidence as untrusted; enforce containment and exact identity/provenance, reject forged/stale/foreign inputs, check current authority before effects and recovery, and never equate a plain hash or copied local receipt with transferable authentication.
- **architecture**: One active owner per attempt for lifecycle, graph, seam closure, finding policy and acceptance; reuse existing graph/evaluation_evidence_edges/wiring_closure, StageLifecycle, RunStore and validators with small responsibilities and separate proposed/as-built truth; reject another framework, coordinator, store or arbitrary module-count target.
- **integrability**: Version exact producer/consumer contracts and complete portable artifact/evidence bundles, multiple contribution/proof representation and typed refusal semantics; explicitly classify additive/breaking changes, preserve valid historical reads with deterministic migration and revalidation, and reject silent drift.
- **data-safety**: Keep historical requirements, approvals, collector passes and immutable artifacts unchanged; append revalidation, reconcile interrupted durable operations, reject changed-input replay and preserve completed work; each migration has a bounded rollback and cannot leave two active owners.
- **sre**: Expose bounded traversal, partial/degraded capability and evidence, precise refusals and authorized continuations; fault-injection and duplicate-event tests prove reconciliation and stop unchanged failed retries while valid event waits remain waits.
- **privacy-compliance**: Export only necessary diagnostic and telemetry fields, no secrets/full prompts/transcripts or predecessor private runtime; retain evidence provenance and safe reason codes without copying authentication authority.
- **cost-finops**: Default graph depth 3, explicit traversal/fan-out/time limits, bounded correction and no unnecessary whole-repo scans or worker fan-out; record provider usage/cache availability honestly and deduplicate replay without fabricated estimates.
- **qa**: Require six real/simulated-labeled production-boundary journeys, positive and targeted severed evidence and current candidate binding; consumer unit fixtures, suite counts, skips and synthetic observations cannot establish real host completion.

## Acceptance criteria (→ DoD)
- AC1 Source and coverage: fixtures verify files/modules/symbols/config/contracts/runtime touchpoints against the bound source exactly once, classify missing/ambiguous/unsupported/rejected inputs, and prevent unverified proposals entering decomposition. Default local depth is 3; depth, fan-out, time, parser, language, ambiguity and policy bounds report exact stopping reasons, never false complete; identical inputs yield deterministic semantic results.
- AC2 Decomposition and closure: a current graph fixture derives task ownership from connected impacted subgraphs and verifies every proposed extension before expansion. Every real cut edge and declared runtime seam has exact producer/consumer nodes and symbols, schema/version, both owners, direction/order, distinct positive/severed selectors and source/graph/requirement/Design/Plan/candidate provenance. Public readiness rejects omitted nodes/edges, orphan or duplicate producers/consumers, wrong direction/order, unowned or stale/incomplete rows; declared cardinality is explicit.
- AC3 Realized conformance: proposed overlays never become as-built truth without an integrated realization. Combined-diff tests expose added, missing/removed, changed, reversed, stale and unexpected seams, including two individually passing task diffs whose shared contract mismatches. Engineering approval blocks until current conformance and every required seam proof are consumed; swapping overlay identity or removing one evidence binding changes the public result to blocked.
- AC4 Boundary proof fidelity: each required worker/task/phase/runtime seam passes via actual upstream production output and a distinct one-edge severance makes the same behavior fail observably. Missing, stale, foreign, multi-edge, ceremonial or non-failing severance is rejected. Consumer-only hand-built fixtures cannot satisfy boundary claims; missing/unexecutable runtime probes remain explicit blocking gaps.
- AC5 Standalone and compatibility: Review without Plan derives current seams from the reviewed source/diff without inventing Plan approval and visibly labels declared/derived, partial, timed-out, unsupported and truncated coverage. Fixtures read incumbent graph, evaluation_evidence_edges and wiring-closure records through deterministic eligible migration/revalidation and reject a second graph/seam/evidence authority.
- AC6 Retained entry and identity obligations: supported launcher fixtures cover ordinary CLI and native hook dispatch preserving selected valid engine, arguments, contract and enforcement; absent/invalid/ambiguous installed candidates fail with a named reason before effects. Distinct runs and replacement attempts use distinct native names, review children bind their owner attempt, exact replay keeps its identity, and a real native spawn accepts a fresh dispatch. Broken dispatch binding or foreign prior results fails without widening authority.
- AC7 C admissibility: a public collection fixture with valid observed bytes and reported pass plus a canonical policy-blocking unresolved finding or missing required evidence cannot pass. Non-blocking debt/observations remain visible without false regression escalation; equal classification yields equal decisions across applicable consumers. Resolution needs current linked evidence. Relied-on historical passes receive append-only revalidation; unavailable evidence is unverified and originals remain unchanged.
- AC8 E truthful recovery: a public missing-terminal fixture names the attempt and observation/wait/reconciliation action, never generic handoff repair. Refusals expose safe reason, committed/observed/pending/uncertain state, exact permitted continuation and prerequisites. Waiting, artifact correction, setup repair and authority request are distinguishable and rechecked on execution. An unchanged retry preserves attempt/completed work; repeated identical failure without progress stops automatic retry and returns the precise blocker while legitimate waits use the named event mechanism.
- AC9 A complete producer bundle: the normal quality-enabled Design path produces and seals actual strategy bytes plus required produced/inherited artifacts. A fresh Plan and Build consumes only that package through public interfaces, with no predecessor runtime access or inserted artifact/receipt/permission. Removing, altering or staling the strategy or another required artifact prevents producer completion or successor admission with the specific boundary reason.
- AC10 B recoverable attempt: parameterized owner/reviewer and applicable-phase fault injection at every relevant local persistence and external-effect boundary never yields readiness with missing/inconsistent records. Same operation replay repairs setup or returns its prior valid result; changed input under that identity is refused. Uncertain external dispatch is reconciled against observed host work before a new launch; unavailable reconciliation remains a named blocker, without an exactly-once remote claim.
- AC11 D contributions and proofs: public Plan/Build/acceptance fixtures support one task contributing to multiple criteria, multiple contributors to one criterion, and multiple distinct exact proof requirements. Accountable acceptance ownership does not exclude contributors. Missing any contribution/proof, stale candidate evidence or required joint integration proof blocks acceptance; all task completions alone cannot imply requirement acceptance. Different shell commands are never guessed equivalent.
- AC12 J1 real native journey: an authorized real host run observes actual start/identity and terminal event and collects genuine output through production interfaces; absent identity or terminal evidence yields an exact refusal and no fabricated completion or duplicate launch. Unsupported host capability is detected before readiness and records unverified with its reason; simulation or skip cannot satisfy this real-host condition.
- AC13 J2 quality journey: record a fresh-execution Design to Plan to Build trace satisfying AC9, with actual producer-selected strategy and quality authority, and the corresponding removed/altered-artifact failure. Record host mode and evidence origin; simulated host fault tests are explicitly simulated and do not stand in for J1 or J6.
- AC14 J3 interruption journey: production-entry replay traces satisfy AC10 across owner/reviewer roles and applicable phases, fresh pickup, duplicate events, cancellation and interruption; each trace labels real or simulated host evidence, retains the same logical operation identity, and asserts readiness records plus launch counts at each boundary.
- AC15 J4 review/correction journey: production collection refuses a pass label with a genuine blocker, routes a bounded authorized correction, and advances only a corrected candidate with sufficient current evidence. Unchanged failure preserves attempt and completed work, and terminates blind retries as AC8 requires. Real/simulated evidence and unresolved gaps are labeled.
- AC16 J5 contribution journey: an actual Plan-produced multiple-task, multiple-proof package reaches Build and aggregate acceptance, including joint behavior evidence; deleting any required contribution/proof or changing candidate identity blocks the same journey. No fixture rewrites the producer package to bypass AC11; each run records real/simulated mode.
- AC17 J6 finalization journey: on a real supported host, production Build output supplies accessible full evidence through fresh Evaluate, Engineering review, scoped human sign-off where required, Retro and declared Publish/final outcome. Missing downstream evidence or applicable authority blocks at its boundary; Build-only completion, document output, synthetic terminal events and skips cannot satisfy this journey. Publication uses PR-based delivery and separately authorized publishing, never direct push to main or historical approval.
- AC18 Authority and history: fixtures distinguish draft/interrupted output, mechanical validation and attributed authorization under the same explicit policy for each supported path; scope-valid authorization may be reused only as recorded and recovery rechecks it. Imported predecessor conversations/runtime, old Plan, approvals, review results, completion claims and stale fingerprints cannot authorize the new effort. Original R-0004 and its R-0001/R-0003 dependencies remain retained; P14 native canary and P23 whole-suite adjudication stay separately open and are not closed by these journeys.
- AC19 Modest reuse and migration: Design review maps each proposed unit to one responsibility, owned inputs/outputs, existing mechanism and current acceptance verification; each bounded migration PR identifies retired/inactive paths and rollback. An extension fixture adds a phase using existing capabilities through definition/processor/domain validation without changing dispatch, pickup, storage, hooks or shared lifecycle. Two active progression owners for one attempt, unexplained new infrastructure or parallel authority fails review; old formats retain explicit compatibility without silently reinterpreting authority.
- AC20 Honest telemetry: replay/duplicate-event fixtures count the attempt and usage once and retain run/phase/attempt/operation/candidate identity, continuation, start/end/elapsed/terminal outcome, last progress/wait reason, correction/replay counts, uncertain effects, evidence gaps and actual next action. Provider usage includes source and cache semantics; absent values remain unavailable, never zero. Sensitive prompts/transcripts/secrets are absent from exported diagnostics and telemetry.
- AC21 Product boundary and fresh handoff: this artifact and canonical requirement record are the only current Product deliverables; no Design/Plan/Build/release/publication or self-approval occurs here. Future Design must revalidate current source, map every criterion and six journeys to owned exact positive/negative selectors, settle approval/compatibility/rollout decisions and preserve exclusions before its applicable gates; legacy Design artifacts are explicitly historical only.
