# Define the product outcome

Inspect the request, existing product decisions and material dependencies.
Define the intended users/outcome, scope, explicit exclusions and observable
acceptance criteria with stable IDs. Reuse accepted decisions; ask only about
missing information that affects the outcome or authorized effects.

Follow [the shared flow](../tp-go/references/shared-flow.md) for the selected
Product route. Initial scope needs material boundaries and the current output,
not every future source/test/evidence filename. Record unresolved dependencies
and validation needs honestly. A source graph is optional supporting evidence.

Present the concrete Product result for separate approval before Design. A
Product-only request ends with that result and does not authorize implementation.
Use a durable brief and relevant references; no delivery receipt or dashboard
opening is required.
