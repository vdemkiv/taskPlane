# Core delivery requirements

Taskplane carries software work from outcome to verified result across Codex and
Claude while preserving the user's scope and existing decisions.

1. Keep Product → Design → Plan → Build → Evaluate → Engineering → Retro and a
   separate actual human approval for each concrete result. Interpret intent in
   context; retain provenance, target, chronology and replay validation.
2. Start with material boundaries and current output. Plan fixes executable Build
   tasks, exact paths, relevant inputs, owners, prerequisites and meaningful checks.
3. Resume with a durable brief and ordinary evidence references. Do not require
   attention receipts, byte accounting, startup probes or conversation replay.
4. Allow root/serial/useful native ownership. Preserve explicitly requested
   independence, actual native identity and terminal/cancellation evidence.
5. Keep ordinary reads available while sealed. Enforce approved structured writes,
   safe paths, ownership and conflicts. Disclose opaque-shell/host coverage limits.
6. Reuse based on relevant dependencies, not whole-workspace bytes or progress
   revisions. Unrelated files need no maintenance acknowledgement.
7. Validate concrete results once on direct submission. Optional prevalidation,
   graph, dashboard and telemetry do not authorize or veto phases.
8. Preserve immutable verification attempts; later applicable fail/unknown cannot
   fall back to old pass. Human acceptance does not change observed check status.
9. Preserve durable atomic/CAS state, native locking and authentic import/recovery
   provenance. Native lock failure refuses before entering a critical section.
10. Keep Stop advisory, including missing or stale phase evidence. Ending a turn
    does not accept or advance a phase; the controller enforces those actions and
    actual mutations. Do not maintain a second Stop retry or approval ledger.
    Keep candidate packages, installed hosts and live acceptance distinct.

Reviews/design do not implement. Remove obsolete tests/fixtures/callers with
removed machinery and retain valuable behavior. Unknown evidence remains visible
with an owner. See [shared flow](../skills/tp-go/references/shared-flow.md).
